M.A. thesis · University of Haifa · 2026

The Sacred in the Poetry of the First World War

Wilfred Owen and Siegfried Sassoon

A close reading of the religious language, divine imagery and prayer in the war poems of Wilfred Owen and Siegfried Sassoon. The thesis argues that neither poet abandons the sacred. Owen rebuilds it as a worldly ethic of compassion and witness, while Sassoon holds it unresolved, between the altar he overturns and the need to kneel he cannot suppress.

Keywords

  • First World War poetry
  • Religion and the sacred
  • Divine imagery
  • Prayer and faith

Academic conferences

  • Dec 2021
    “Boulevard of Dreams”English Language and Literature Conference · University of Haifa

    A talk on postcolonial literature and on literature as a form of culture. It looked at how writers from formerly colonised places tell their own stories, and how a literary text carries the memory, language and identity of the culture it comes from.

    Postcolonialism / Identity / Literature and culture

  • Jun 2021
    “The Future: Between Imagination and Reality”Teaching and Instruction Sciences Conference · University of Haifa

    A talk on how people imagine the future, from prophecy and visionary writing to the futures that science fiction once predicted. It asked which imagined futures came true, which did not, and what that means for preparing learners for a world that keeps changing.

    Prophecy / Imagination and reality / Education

  • May 2021
    “Translation: The Last Dance”English Language and Literature Conference · University of Haifa

    A talk on literary translation as a dance between two languages. It looked at what a translator keeps, loses and reinvents when a text moves from one culture to another, and why the same poem can read so differently in translation.

    Translation / Language and culture

  • Apr 2021
    “In Every Difficulty Lies an Opportunity Waiting for You”Teaching, Learning and Instruction Conference · University of Haifa

    A talk on resilience in learning. It looked at how difficulties in the classroom can become chances for both students and teachers to grow, and how a teacher can help learners see a challenge as a starting point rather than an ending.

    Resilience / Teaching and learning

  • Jun 2020
    “Social Media: Connecting with the World or Disconnecting from Reality?”English Teaching Conference · University of Haifa

    A talk on social media in young people's lives. It weighed the ways social networks connect us to the world against the ways they can pull us away from real life, and asked how English teachers can use them well in the classroom.

    Social media / Digital literacy / English teaching

  • Nov 2019
    “Echo: Dover Beach”English Language and Literature Conference · University of Haifa

    A talk on Matthew Arnold's “Dover Beach”, read alongside the Romantic tradition. It followed the poem's longing and its landscape of sea, cliffs and fading light, and how the retreating “Sea of Faith” becomes an image of a world losing its certainties.

    Romanticism / Longing / Landscape / Faith
