Research · Cybersecurity

Intrusion Detection: a Critical Evaluation

I reproduced a popular NSL-KDD tutorial that reports 98–99% accuracy and showed why those results don't hold up. The tutorial cross-validated on its test set, leaked preprocessing between train and test data, and relied on accuracy with imbalanced classes. My evaluation trains on the training set, tests on the test set, and reports balanced accuracy, macro-F1, MCC and per-class recall.

Python · scikit-learn · ML evaluation
