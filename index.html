I reproduced a popular NSL-KDD tutorial that reports 98–99% accuracy and showed why those numbers don't hold up on genuinely unseen data.

  • Held-out recall for rare attacks (R2L, U2R) drops below 10%
  • Reports balanced accuracy, macro-F1, MCC and per-class recall
